Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

InfennonLabs LLC ("InfennonLabs," "we," "us") makes simple learning games for everyone, including children. This policy explains what information our website at infennonlabs.com and our mobile app (together, the "Services") handle. This policy is addressed to parents and guardians. If a child uses the Services, the parent or guardian is responsible for that use and should review this policy and our Terms of Service on the child's behalf.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us. Our games do not require you to provide any personal information. In the event you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write, and we use it only to reply. Only a parent or guardian should email us. If we learn that a child has emailed us, we will make every reasonable effort to delete the email from our systems.

Information collected automatically. When you use the Services, some technical information is collected by the services that deliver them:

  • Hosting and security (Cloudflare). Our website is delivered through Cloudflare, which processes your IP address and basic request details (browser type, pages requested, date and time) to serve pages, prevent abuse, and keep the site running.
  • App install and usage measurement (AppsFlyer). Our mobile app uses AppsFlyer, a service provider, to count installs and to understand, in aggregate, how the app is used: when the app is opened, which game is opened, roughly how long a game is played (in ranges such as under 1 minute, 1 to 5 minutes, 5 to 15 minutes, and over 15 minutes), and whether the home screen's filters and Study Plan are used. We do not use it to identify a child, and these counts are not tied to a name, an account, or a profile. We configure it for children's apps: we do not collect or use the iOS advertising identifier (IDFA) or the Android advertising ID, we do not show an App Tracking Transparency prompt, we block sharing of data with advertising partners, and we turn on AppsFlyer's anonymization setting. AppsFlyer still receives technical information from the device, such as the IP address (which may be masked), device model and operating system version, language and country, screen size, mobile carrier codes, the types of sensors the device has, app version, and a random install identifier that AppsFlyer generates.

Information stored on your device. Some games save information on your device so they pick up where you left off. This includes preferences such as sound on or off, audio support, or difficulty level, and in some games the state of your game (for example, how a farm is laid out). It is kept in your browser's local storage or in the app's storage on your device, and it is not sent to us. A parent or guardian can clear it at any time from the browser or device settings, which also resets the games.

2. How we use information

  • To run, secure, and troubleshoot the Services.
  • To answer emails you send us.
  • To understand how many people install and use the app, so we can improve it.
  • To follow the law and protect our rights.

We do not build profiles of users.

3. Who receives information

We do not sell personal information. Information is handled by the service providers that help us run the Services:

ProviderWhy
CloudflareWebsite delivery and security

If you download our mobile app from an app store (such as the Apple App Store, Google Play, or the Amazon Appstore), the store collects its own information about downloads and use under its own privacy policy, which we do not control.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect people's safety or our rights, or as part of a sale or merger of our business. These providers have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices.

4. Advertising

The Services do not show advertising, and our website does not load advertising code from any ad network. If that ever changes, we will update this policy first.

5. Children's privacy

Our games are made for people of all ages, including young children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the Services do not have accounts, chat, or any way to post public content. Our mobile app does not use advertising identifiers. We do not show advertising, and we design the Services to comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has sent us personal information (for example, by emailing us), contact us at the address below and we will delete it.

6. Security and retention

We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no system is perfectly secure. We do not keep user accounts or profiles. Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to respond. Data held by Cloudflare and AppsFlyer follows their retention practices.

7. Your choices and rights

Because we do not have accounts, there is no profile for us to access or delete. Still, a parent or guardian can contact us at privacy@infennonlabs.com to ask what we hold about them or their child, and to correct or delete anything we have (such as past emails). We will respond within 45 days, and we may need to confirm a few details, including that you are the child's parent or guardian, to find the right record.

Depending on where you live (for example, California and other U.S. states, the European Economic Area, or the United Kingdom), you may have the legal right to access, correct, delete, or port your information, to object to or restrict its use, and to complain to your local privacy regulator. We do not sell personal information, and we do not treat people differently for exercising their rights.

8. International transfers

We are based in the United States, and our providers may process information in the United States and other countries. Where required, we rely on safeguards such as standard contractual clauses that our providers offer.

9. Third-party links

The Services may link to other websites or apps. We do not control them and are not responsible for their privacy practices. Parents and guardians should review those practices before a child uses them.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Services change, for example if we add new tools. We will post the new version here with a new effective date, and for material changes we will give a more visible notice on our website. Parents and guardians should review this policy from time to time.
