Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

InfennonLabs LLC ("InfennonLabs," "we," "us") makes simple learning games for everyone, including children. This policy explains what information our website at infennonlabs.com and our mobile app (together, the "Services") handle.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us. Our games do not require you to provide any personal information. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write, and we use it only to reply.

Information collected automatically. When you use the Services, some technical information is collected by the services that deliver them:

  • Hosting and security (Cloudflare). Our website is delivered through Cloudflare, which processes your IP address and basic request details (browser type, pages requested, date and time) to serve pages, prevent abuse, and keep the site running.
  • Fonts (Google Fonts). Our website loads its typeface from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests the font file from Google and Google sees your IP address.
  • Advertising code (Google AdSense). Our website loads Google AdSense code, which may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details.

Information stored on your device. Some games save information on your device so they pick up where you left off. This includes preferences such as sound on or off, audio support, or difficulty level, and in some games the state of your game (for example, how a farm is laid out). It is kept in your browser's local storage or in the app's storage on your device, and it is not sent to us. You can clear it at any time from your browser or device settings, which also resets the games.

2. How we use information

  • To run, secure, and troubleshoot the Services.
  • To answer emails you send us.
  • To follow the law and protect our rights.

We do not build profiles of users.

3. Who receives information

We do not sell personal information. Information is handled by the service providers that help us run the Services:

ProviderWhy

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect people's safety or our rights, or as part of a sale or merger of our business. These providers have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices.

4. Advertising

The Services do not currently show advertising, but our website may start serving ads in the future. Google is our advertising provider, and its use of information is described in Google's Privacy Policy. If we add advertising, we will update this policy first. For children's use, we will only use child-appropriate, non-personalized advertising and advertising signals where the law allows it.

5. Children's privacy

Our games are made for people of all ages, including young children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the Services do not have accounts, chat, or any way to post public content. Our mobile app does not use advertising identifiers. We make every effort to limit and block personalized advertising on our website, and we design the Services to comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has sent us personal information (for example, by emailing us), contact us at the address below and we will delete it.

6. Security and retention

We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no system is perfectly secure. We do not keep user accounts or profiles. Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to respond and for our records. Data held by Cloudflare and Google follows their retention practices.

7. Your choices and rights

Because we do not have accounts, there is no profile for us to access or delete. Still, you can contact us at privacy@infennonlabs.com to ask what we hold about you, to correct or delete anything we have (such as past emails). We will respond within 45 days, and we may need to confirm a few details to find the right record.

Depending on where you live (for example, California and other U.S. states, the European Economic Area, or the United Kingdom), you may have the legal right to access, correct, delete, or port your information, to object to or restrict its use, and to complain to your local privacy regulator. We do not sell personal information, and we do not treat people differently for exercising their rights.

8. International transfers

We are based in the United States, and our providers may process information in the United States and other countries. Where required, we rely on safeguards such as standard contractual clauses that our providers offer.

9. Third-party links

The Services may link to other websites or apps. We do not control them and are not responsible for their privacy practices.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Services change, for example if we add new tools or advertising. We will post the new version here with a new effective date, and for material changes we will give a more visible notice.
